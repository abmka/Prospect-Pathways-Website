This is where we will post the things partners, landlords and residents ask us about most
often: new properties coming online, vacancies we are recruiting for, changes to how referrals
work, and what we are learning from the people we house.

## What you will find here

- **Vacancies and availability** — when a property opens or rooms become available in a
  particular area.
- **Recruitment** — new roles, and what it is actually like to work here.
- **Service updates** — changes to referral routes, out-of-hours arrangements, or how we
  report to commissioners.
- **What we are learning** — the things that come out of resident feedback and our own
  reviews, including where we have got something wrong.

## Who it is for

Mostly local authority housing teams, probation and prison release teams, hospital discharge
teams, and voluntary sector partners across Birmingham. Landlords considering leasing a
property to us will find the practical detail here too.

If there is something you would find useful that we are not covering, tell us and we will
write about it.
